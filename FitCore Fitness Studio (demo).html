Our story

FitCore began when three coaches got tired of watching newcomers quit within a month. Gyms felt loud, confusing and built for people who already knew what to do.

So we built the opposite: a studio where the first session is a conversation, not a sales pitch.

Mission: help ordinary people build strength they can keep for life.

How to start going to the gym as a beginner

Starting is the hardest part. A simple plan removes most of the fear.

Pick a small schedule

Train three days a week for 45 minutes. Add more only when this feels easy.

Learn five movements

Squat, hinge, push, pull and carry cover most of what you need.

Ask for help

Coaches expect questions. Book an intro session and bring your goals.

Conclusion: Start small and repeat. Book a free trial to begin.

Five common workout mistakes and how to avoid them

Most setbacks come from a few fixable habits.

1. Skipping the warm-up

Five minutes of light movement prepares joints and muscles.

2. Lifting too heavy too soon

Choose a weight that lets you finish every rep with good form.

3. Changing plans every week

Follow one programme for at least six weeks.

4. Ignoring rest

Muscles grow during recovery. Sleep and rest days count as training.

5. Training without a goal

Write one measurable target, such as a first push-up.

Conclusion: Fix one mistake at a time. Ask a coach to review your routine.

How to build a sustainable fitness routine

The best routine is the one you can keep doing.

Anchor it to your day

Pair training with a fixed time, like right after work.

Plan for bad weeks

Keep a 20-minute fallback session for busy days.

Track what matters

Note sessions completed, not just weight lost.

Find support

Group classes and coaches make showing up easier.

Conclusion: Consistency is built, not found. See membership plans.
